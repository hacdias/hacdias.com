October has been incredibly hectic. I've redesigned this website, I've been to two concerts and one comedy show, spent a few days in Amsterdam, I've written maybe way too many posts, and there's so much more happening. Let's recap!

<!--more-->

## ✍️ Website {#website}

After a dormant summer, this website is now seeing much more activity. Earlier this month, I introduced a new [design](/2026/10/13/redesign/), which I've tweaked slightly here and there since: less on-the-nose link underlines, improved inline code rendering, and some other things here and there. I am much happier with this design than with the previous iteration!

I've also released a few more posts than usual. Some of them had been sitting as ideas in my drafts, so I just thought: why not? I've also updated Eagle to allow for scheduling posts, so that's nice. I've also revisited the existing pages to make sure everything was up to date, and re-introduced the [projects](/projects/) page, which had been gone for years.

If you end up lurking in my website's [repository](https://github.com/hacdias/hacdias.com), you may notice that the history now extends back to the original commit. When I re-open-sourcered my website [back in 2023](/2023/11/18/open-sourcering-my-website/) I had decided to only have the Git history since the moment I started using Hugo again. But after a bit of back and forth with Claude, I managed to get a copy of the history without any private posts or unmoderated comments, so that's nice.

## 🍄 Life {#life}

If you've opened this website recently, you might've noticed I'm searching for a new job. The company I work for is sort of winding down in its current shape from January onwards, which means I need to find a new adventure. It is bittersweet since I do enjoy what we are working on and I was hoping to stay longer in my current role.

Over the last month I've sent out almost 20 applications. Some rejected me and two replied; one of them ended up rejecting me at a later stage. For the rest I'm still waiting to hear back. If you know anyone hiring and think I could be a great fit, let me know! My resume is also [publicly available](/resume/). I'm mostly looking at developer tooling, open-source, and remote-first companies.

Lots of other things have happened this month, but I've been too busy to keep track of everything. There was my birthday, which I barely celebrated due to all of the busyness; we also carved some pumpkins with friends in Utrecht, which was fun; and there's one more piece of news I'm excited to share soon.

## 💪 Health {#health}

Generally going well. I finished my swimming lessons earlier this month and got [the last diploma](/2026/10/15/swimming/). I've been going swimming regularly since, and I still want to decide if and when I want to join a club, because I also like the idea of having some guidance and people to talk to.

## 🍿 Entertainment {#entertainment}

A part of the hustle from this month was also due to [live shows](/logs/performances/). We spent a few days in Amsterdam where we were planning to go to three concerts, but, unfortunately, one of them ended up getting canceled. Nevertheless, it was amazing to see both *Allie X* and *Slayyyter*. And just a few days ago we saw *Derek Mitchell* with his second iteration of the *Double Dutch* comedy show.

When it comes to [movies](/logs/movies/), we did not watch that many. We went twice to the cinema and those were the only films we watched. We first went to *Toutes Directions*, which is a Dutch film that a colleague's father starred in. It was quite fun.

And while we were in Amsterdam, we also watched *Digger*. The fun thing about it is that we ended up watching it in the second row and, if you've watched this movie, you can imagine how the perspective from which many of the scenes are shot ends up being incredibly funny.

When it comes to [series](/logs/shows/), we did watch quite a few things. We started watching *Industry* without high hopes, but we've flown through seasons 1, 2, and 3 and are now busy with season 4. And we also finished the latest seasons of *Slow Horses* and *Ted Lasso*.

I hope the coming months will be a bit more stable but, knowing what I know now, I don't think that'll be the case! But I'm hoping for the best. Have a nice November!
