We all use apps and services from large corporations and I'd bet that most of us also pay for some of those. What we more often forget are the small apps and services maintained by one or just a couple of people.

<!--more-->

There are some apps from small developers that I've been using for years, and also paying for them, even if it's in a donation mode. I've noticed that I have much more empathy, compassion and willingness to pay for something that is developed by a single person or a very small team. Today I want to focus on one of these: [Liftin'](https://www.liftinapp.co/).

Liftin' is my app of choice for strength workouts. I've been using this app since September 2024. Before that, I had been using [GymBook](/2023/10/14/gymbook-a-simple-and-easy-gym-app/), also made by a small team, which worked extremely well during the year or so that I used it. However, when I discovered Liftin', I ended up moving apps.

The Apple Watch integration was one of the reasons I moved over: it just felt native, without hassle, and integrated very nicely with the rest of the UI. I also don't need to open the app on the phone anymore for the workout to show up in Apple Health.

Even though when I started using this app it had fewer features than GymBook, over the years it has gotten better and gained more features. Feature parity has, as far as I know, long been achieved.

The app is generally very sleek and minimalist, and has every function that I need for strength workouts: timers, warmup calculator, automatic progression, and integration with Apple Health. Nowadays it even provides an MCP server, which can be nice if you use an LLM to iterate on some of the routines.

If you're looking for a gym app, and you use iOS, I would definitely recommend giving Liftin' a try. It is minimal, it looks good, it gets frequent updates, and it works well!
