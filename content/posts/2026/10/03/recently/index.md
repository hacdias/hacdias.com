September has been an extremely eventful month, especially the past weeks, and I'm sure it doesn't stop here. The next two months will probably also be a bit chaotic. While I'm not yet ready to share all of it, let's take a look at some of the things that happened in September.

<!--more-->

## 🍄 Life {#life}

![Official Residency of the Portuguese Ambassador](image:2026-10-03-recently-01)
{width="4000" height="3000" class="left"}

At the beginning of September, my Portuguese university and the Portuguese embassy organized an event for alumni in The Netherlands. The event was held at the Official Residency of the Ambassador, so not at the embassy itself. It was fun to go, even though I didn't know anyone, but I always forget how *far* The Hague is. Since the event started at 17:00 sharp, I had to leave around 15:00 to be on time.

Sometime this month there was another electricity outage in my building and in the surrounding buildings. Just like the last time, it was cause by construction workers damaging an electricity cable. I think it's fine and good that so much is being built at the moment, but please, be careful. It's the second time in two months.

![They Knew They F* Up The Cable](image:2026-10-03-recently-02)
{width="3024" height="4032" class="right"}

Talking about construction, they came this month to check if the walls were sufficiently dry to repare since we had a leakage almost two months ago. It's almost dry, except one specific wall made of a specific material. That needs to wait a bit longer. They'll come later in October to fix the plaster.

Also about plaster, I had told the landlord that all of the inside walls had damaged plaster next to the ceiling. This is normal considering it's a relatively new building and high rise. Since they hadn't fixed it yet, it was looking a bit ugly so I asked them to come fix it and said it was *all* of the inside walls.

The "all" didn't land and they booked someone for one and a half hours, which meant he could only fix one room. At least that room looks very nice now. They said someone would call me to make an appointment to do the rest of the apartment, yet another company. No call yet.

Uh, for something more positive, we've also been going to this bakery on our neighbourhood - one of those that is only open 3 times a week - to get bread frequently and sometimes some pastries. It's delicious!

## 💪 Health {#health}

Everything is going well. I'm swimming regularly, and my swimming lessons are almost coming to an end. That also means I need to see what I want to do afterwards. I want to keep swimming, but not always alone. But that's a problem for later.

I was also sick last week, marking the beginning of the viruses season. Other than that, I think it's all fine.

## 📚 Reading {#reading}

I finished reading *Recursion*, after a long long time. When I picked up this book, I decided to read it on my way to and from the office, which meant that I would only read it once a week. With the summer, I happened to not go as often to the office because there were no other people, so it took a looong time.

The book is really enjoyable. I can also see that it is from the same author as *Dark Matter*. Not sure if that is a good, or a bad thing, but there were certain things that felt like a "formula" or maybe just a coincidence. Nevertheless, very interesting concept, would recommend!

## 🍿 Entertainment {#entertainment}

This past month we finished the last season of *Silo*, which I really enjoyed. For someone that read the books, some things did not come as a surprise, but sometimes I wish they were a bit more fast paced in the first episodes. However, I understand that the series needs to work on a different way than the books. You can't just leave the main character out for a full season like the second book did.

In the series department, we also binged *Hacks*, which was quite funny, and watched the first season of *DTF St. Louis*, which I learn afterwards is a dark comedy series.

When it comes to movies, I watched *The Invite* at the beginning of September. It was nice, I would recommend if comedy drama is your thing. We also saw *Leviticus* and *A Nice Indian Boy*, the first a bit scary, the second funny.
