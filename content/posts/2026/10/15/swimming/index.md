Today was the day: I got my National Swimming Diploma C, the last of the three diplomas to reach the national swimming safety norms in The Netherlands. It's been an incredible journey, and I'm very curious about what's going to come from this.

<!--more-->

I [started swimming lessons](/2026/01/16/swimming/) back in January, after enrolling for the third time. The previous times, there was a waiting list, so I kept postponing it. In April I got my [Diploma A](/2026/04/21/swimming-diploma/), in July my [Diploma B](/2026/07/09/swimming/), and that same month I also did the [Zwem4daagse](/2026/07/17/zwem4daagse/), where I swam 500 meters every day for four days.

Since then, I've done all the lessons for Diploma C, and I've also swum 500 meters in a row without stopping. I've been going to the swimming pool every Monday, where I swim around a kilometer, and then having my lessons on Thursdays.

![National Swimming Diploma C](image:2026-10-15-swimming)
{width="4000" height="3000"}

I had not expected to get the last diploma within a year. It hasn't even been a year: it's been ten months, with vacations in the middle, so in practice it was much less than that. I've learned a lot, I'm much more comfortable in the water, and I can now swim three different styles, all except butterfly. Even my front crawl is so much better than it was back when I was in the swimming club at school, way too many years ago.

I'm thankful to a lot of people. To my boyfriend, who sort of pushed me to do this. To everyone at work, who were very supportive and incredibly flexible, so I could also take lessons during the day. And to all the teachers who taught me over the past months.

So, what's next? I don't know yet. There are so many possibilities. The swimming pools don't offer more lessons, but the clubs do. If I want to keep going in the direction of the lessons I've been having, with survival and technique, then I should do *zwemvaardigheid* (swimming proficiency), which also has three diplomas. There's also *reddingszwemmen* (lifesaving). It would be very nice, because you learn a lot of different things, but I don't know if I want to keep having lessons where I have to swim fully clothed.

So I might go for *trimzwemmen*, which is more about working on your condition. Since it's with a coach and a group of people, I assume they also help you with technique. I just need to decide where. For the next month, I'll do my search, send a few emails, and see which club in Eindhoven fits my goals best. They all seem to have trial lessons, so I can always just go try!

In the meanwhile, I'll keep going to the swimming pool twice a week. I really like being in the swimming pool. It's just such a different feeling. You listen to the water, you listen to yourself, and you have to adapt to the constraints: you can't breathe whenever you want, you can't move the way you normally would. It's a calming feeling.

I don't know when I'll give another update about swimming. Maybe in the next month, once I know what I'm going to do. For now, I'm just going to keep swimming, and I'm thankful for the whole journey until now!
