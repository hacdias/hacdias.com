August is now coming to an end and, with it, the summer. This has been an unusually hot summer in the Netherlands, and I'm quite happy we're now approaching colder times. This month wasn't as eventful as I thought it was, but let's recap.

## 🍄 Life {#life}

At the beginning of the month, I went to the Canal Pride Parade in Amsterdam. I had the opportunity to be in a boat this year, which completely changes how you experience the pride parade. It was much more enjoyable.

I've been to Amsterdam before for the Canal Pride but I have never enjoyed it that much, especially in comparison to Utrecht's, or to normal parades, which, in the Netherlands, are called "walks". On regular parades where you walk you are part of it. In Utrecht, in their canal parade, you're also quite close to the boats, feeling much more personal than what it feels to watch the canal parade in Amsterdam.

A week later, a friend visited, which was quite nice. He saw a bit more of Eindhoven than the last time and we biked quite a lot. At the same time, we were "fighting" with an electricity issue, which I've [written about](/2026/08/25/electricity-leakage/), as well as a water leakage, which I still want to write about.

## 💪 Health {#health}

At the beginning of the month I had an appointment with the hand surgeon and we now decided to get an MRI to see if we can see what's wrong. Two years of conservative physiotherapy has helped reduce the pain, and my wrist is measurably much stronger than it has ever been, but it still hurts in some positions. MRI has been done, and in a week and a half I have an appointment to discuss it.

When it comes to swimming, I managed to swim 500m front crawl without stopping last week. It was quite nice. I do feel like it's getting easier without me getting as tired, which likely means that technique is getting a bit better. On that topic, my swimming lessons have resumed, and I'm now headed for the last diploma of the Swim-ABC.

The teacher mentioned last class that I can basically do everything for the last diploma, so I can focus on whatever I want until the diploma moment. I've thought a bit about it and I think just focusing on technique is the best route for now. I want to make sure I'm swimming efficiently.

## 📚 Reading {#reading}

I'm still reading *Recursion* at a very slow tempo, not because I don't like it, but because I haven't had that much time to dedicate to it. I've also realized that reading books in one sitting is probably not the best, since I will quickly forget them. So reading them slowly will probably help with long term retention.

## 🍿 Entertainment {#entertainment}

I went twice to the cinema this month, meaning that my Cineville subscription was definitely worth it this month 😅. We first went to *The Odyssey* because every single person was talking about it. I've heard quite some criticism saying that they do not explain the background and meaning of some of the mythology, but I didn't feel that at all? Maybe my little background knowledge was enough.

We also went to *Jim Queen*, which was the most ridiculously funny film I've watched in the past months. It's a French animation movie about a virus that turns gay people straight. I went completely blind without knowing what to expect and it was... just... funny!

Also this past month we finished the last season of *Not Suitable for Work*, *Adults*, *Euphoria* and *House of the Dragon*. I don't really have much to say about them. The first two were funny, comedy. The third I should've probably paid more attention. And the last was better than the previous season, I feel like much more has happened.
