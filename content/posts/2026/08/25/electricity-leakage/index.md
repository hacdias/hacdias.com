A month ago I [wrote](/2026/07/21/electricity-leakage/) about an electricity problem I had while Chris was gone. At the time, the technician couldn't find the origin of the problem, and I ended up the post saying that "the problem might come back again". Guess what? It did.

<!--more-->

It's now two weeks since we've got it fully resolved, but it was a mess. On the 4th of August, I woke up, went to the bathroom: light didn't turn on. Immediately went to the fridge. Again, no electricity in the exact same groups as two weeks before.

I went immediately check all the switches and... the same thing had happened: the ground switch had tripped. I think I called it a main switch the last time, but that's not what it was. I turned off group 5 - the problematic group -, turned on the ground switch, and then group 5. Tripped again.

![The Outage of the Sun](image:2026-08-25-electricity-leakage-3)
{width="4000" height="3000"}

It was still early, so there was no one I could call. So I wanted a bit. I started by calling the company that had been here the last time. They said they couldn't come because their assignment was marked as finished, and that the technician had said that the problem was solved. Hmm. Sure.

Then, I called the landlord company. They set out an assignment and someone came the same day. I will spare you the details: it wasn't that easy. I had to call multiple people multiple times to make sure that the communication between all these companies was being properly done, which it wasn't. If I hadn't called, they would've not come the same day.

Someone came that day and he checked the same cables as the other guy. He found 3 cables that were "leaking" electricity somehow. He thought it was odd. But well... alone he couldn't do end to end tests. So he connected group 5 to a new switch he installed, which would hopefully let us have electricity in that group until he came back with another colleague... another time.

That same night, it tripped again. But this time, the whole apartments electricity went down with it. I don't really know what he installed, but it seemed to be a bypass for the ground switch. Now, instead of the ground switch tripping, the "main" switch in the hall that connects to our apartment tripped. We tried a few more times, but it'd always go down after a random amount of time.

We called the landlord company emergency number and they sent someone that had, of course, absolutely no context about what had been done that day, so he just turned everything on again and said it was fine. He left. I went take a shower. Everything tripped again. We called him and he came back just to connect the fridge with a different plug in the hall. We decided to keep group 5 and the new bypass off. (He had left them on, but it tripped again, of course. What was he thinking.)

Anyways, we had to wait more than a week for them to come fix it. The big problem was that this fell during the construction people vacation, which happens at the same time in the whole country, so all these companies had a lot less people working.

They came on the 13th of August, and after trying of everything and unplugging all the sockets, they found the guilty cable: there was an electricity cable going underground that was wet and burned. So there was just a single electricity leak, *but* apparently the electricity installation of this apartment is a bit too confusing and connected in unexpected ways.

They tried replacing the cable but it didn't work out, since the  new cable wasn't going through the curve under the ground. They ended up connecting this socket to the same group as the dishwasher. Fine, we never connect anything powerful there, and we can just avoid using it while the dishwasher's running. It's now up to the landlord to get it properly fixed if they want, but that would involve opening the wall and the floor, neither of those are things we want.

Anyways: at the same time, we were dealing with a leakage in the drain of the shower. You see the common thing? Floor, water. Water travels far. They say they are not related. I still have my suspicion. Because I saw the cable and it felt quite wet, not just humid. Anyways, the leaking drain will be for another post, maybe. It's not fully fixed, but it's contained, so that's good.

Happy that all electricity is working normally again, but how on earth can this take so long? It was never treated as an emergency because it was partial and not full outage. But like: we had only electricity in the hall, bathroom and one of the bedrooms. Fridge and all other important equipment were connected in the kitchen... I'd rather not have electricity in the bathroom, hall and main bedroom for a week and a half than in the kitchen, office and living room.

Stressful time it was, especially having to mediate the constant miscommunication between all these companies, especially at the beginning. After it reached the company coming for the actual problem, it was fine to communicate. But getting there? A nightmare.
