- **Upper asymptotic bound:** worst-case scenario. Denoted by \\(O\\).
- **Lower asymptotic bound:** best-case scenario. Denoted by \\(\Omega\\).
- **Tight asymptotic bound:** denoted by \\(\theta\\). Iff \\(O(g(n))=\Omega(g(n))\\).
