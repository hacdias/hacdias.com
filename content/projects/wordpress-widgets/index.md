Between 2015 and 2017, Luís Soares and I developed and maintained several WordPress widget plugins, in PHP. The idea of these widgets was to display a user's profile summary from certain platforms on their WordPress website. The plugins have since been archived.

<!--more-->

- [**IMDb Profile Widget**](https://github.com/refactors/imdb-widget): shows your IMDb profile, ratings, watchlist, lists, reviews and board posts.
- [**GitHub Profile Widget**](https://github.com/refactors/github-profile-widget): shows your GitHub profile, followers, repositories, gists and organizations.
- [**HackerRank Profile Widget**](https://github.com/refactors/hackerrank-profile-widget): shows your HackerRank profile, badges, contests, solved challenges and followed discussions, with a light, dark or balanced theme.
