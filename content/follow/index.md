All listing pages, such as any category ([micro](/micro/), [articles](/articles/), etc) and any tag have two feeds attached: a JSON and a RSS one. You can access them by appending `/feed.json` for the JSON feed, or `/feed.xml` for the RSS feed, to the URL.

Example for the [all](/all) page (includes micro and articles):

- **Canonical URL**: [https://hacdias.com/all/](/all/)
- **RSS feed URL**: [https://hacdias.com/all/feed.xml](/all/feed.xml)
- **JSON feed URL**: [https://hacdias.com/all/feed.json](/all/feed.json)
