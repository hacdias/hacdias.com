The main way to follow this blog is using RSS Reader or some app that supports microformats. All listing and tags pages, such as [micro](/micro/) and [articles](/articles/), have two feeds attached: a JSON and an RSS feed. Your RSS reader should pick up the feeds automagically.

You can access them by appending `/feed.json` for the JSON feed, or `/feed.xml` for the RSS feed, to the URL. Here's some for quick reference:

|Page|RSS|JSON|
|----|---|---|
|[All](/all/)|[https://hacdias.com/all/feed.xml](/all/feed.xml)|[https://hacdias.com/all/feed.json](/all/feed.json)|
|[Articles](/articles/)|[https://hacdias.com/articles/feed.xml](/articles/feed.xml)|[https://hacdias.com/articles/feed.json](/articles/feed.json)|
|[Micro](/micro/)|[https://hacdias.com/micro/feed.xml](/micro/feed.xml)|[https://hacdias.com/micro/feed.json](/micro/feed.json)|
