When I first started reading this book and came to the part where Julia appeared I thought: here's one more book with a love story and a happy ending. I was wrong. But that won't change my opinion about this book. That was the best part. The unhappy ending - it wasn't unhappy, it was unusual. The way it ends is a must have for this book. Without it, I wouldn't rate it with 4/5 stars. Its ending shows us that nothing is linear and that we are very vulnerable and that we can change a lot without noticing it. We might be in dangerous without knowing. We might be being controlled without knowing. This books is a must-read for those who like and <i>need</i> to think about our society and our way of living.
