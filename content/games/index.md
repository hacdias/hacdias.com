I am not a person that plays a lot of games. However, there are some games that have a special place in my heart, or that I enjoy playing sometimes. I want this page to be used to track the games I play, or that I have played, as well as some progress information about them.

# PlayStation Portable

The PlayStation Portable (PSP) from Sony was my first console ever, gifted to me by my godmother. At the time, I don’t think my parents thought it was a great idea, but what could they do? I was around 10 years old, maybe a bit younger or a bit older, and I was extremely happy with this purchase.

I must say that the PSP served me well thorough the years until the disk player stopped working properly. I think that was a common issue of the PSP and I ended up selling it for pieces and given most of my games out to my cousin.
