- **Proportion**: 30g beans : 500g water
- **Grind**: medium coarse

**Brew**

1. Grind beans and boil water
2. Put paper filter (3 folds on the spout)
3. Rinse filter with boiling water, dump the water
4. Add groups, make little hole in the center
5. Place on scale and zero it out
6. Add 60-90g water and stir to wet all ground, wait 40 seconds
7. Add water in circular motion, break clumps
8. When 500g water, use spoon to stir clockwise and then anti clockwise
9. Givechemex a little shake
10. Wait
