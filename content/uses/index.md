This is my **uses** page. It is inspired in the [uses.tech](https://uses.tech/) movement and its main goal is to share with you the things I use on my daily life, from [hardware](#hardware) to [software](#software). It might not be a 100% up to date, but I will try to keep an eye on it to keep it alive!

## Hardware

Despite trying to have as less devices as I can, I think I end up having some that are not required to have a functional life.

{{< figure cdn="2021-02-desk" class="full-width" alt="My desk" caption="My desk. Not my best shot" >}}

My desk contains my "essential" devices and everything else I use on a regular basis for work, university or just for entertainment. This encompasses everything from my smartphone to my monitor.

**The essentials** are those devices that I must have to complete my work successfully. Without them, I wouldn't be able to work, or even go to university.

- **Laptop**: 2018 Macbook Pro 13"
- **Smartphone:** iPhone 11 Pro

**The upgrades**, on the other hand, are aimed to increase productivity and well being. These include monitors, stands, speakers, etc:

- **Laptop stand:** Rain mStand
- **Monitor**: Asus VG27A
- **Tablet**: 2018 iPad Pro 11"
- **Keyboard:** Ducky One 2 Mini
- **Mouse:** Asus Strix Impact
- **Dock**: CalDigit TS3 Plus

On the sound department, I have 3 devices:

- **Speakers**: Logitech Z213
- **Headphones:** JBL Live 5000BT
- **Earphones:** Airpods (2nd Gen)

{{< figure cdn="2021-02-nintendo-switch" class="full-width" alt="My Nintendo Switch" caption="My Nintendo Switch" >}}

My desk also features my one and only console: a Nintendo Switch. I live in a studio and I don't have much space. Thus, my desk is both my workplace, and my "TV" for movies and entertainment.

Besides all these devices, I also want to shout out to my:

- **Raspberry Pi 4 Model B**, that runs some services for personal use, some of which are intrinsically related to [this website](/meta); and my
- **Yubikey 5C** x2 for 2FA authentication and GPG signing.

{{< figure cdn="2021-02-secrid-wallet" class="full-width" alt="Secrid" caption="Secrid is my wallet of choice" >}}

The most unexpected thing on this list is my wallet. However, it is so small and so useful that it is complicated not to include it. I have been using a [Secrid](https://secrid.com/) wallet for a few years already and I recommend you that if you don't care anything but a few cards and eventual bills.

### Photography

On the photography department, I currently own a single camera: a Fujifilm X-T30. It is small and very nice. The pictures are splendid. However, it is a mess to edit Fuji RAWs due to the way they're encoded. I might write an article one day about it.

{{< figure class="full-width" cdn="2020-09-fujixt30" alt="Fujifilm X-T30" >}}

Besides the camera itself, I also have two **lenses**:

- Fujifilm XF 18-55mm f/2.8-4.0 OIS
- Fujifilm XF 27mm f2.8 (**coming soon**)

I also own a **tripod**: a Manfrotto Befree.
## Software

- **Code editor:** my all round code editor is [Visual Studio Code][vscode]. For some Go specific projects, I use [Golang][goland]. On the terminal, I prefer [vim]. My font of choice for programming is [Fira Mono][fm].
- **Terminal:** my terminal app on macOS is [iTerm][iterm]. I use [zsh] as my shell with [oh my zsh] installed. This installation is all customized via my [dotfiles].
- **Browser:** Chrome on macOS and Safari or Chrome on iOS.
- **Mail and calendar:** I use [Spark](https://sparkmailapp.com/) as both my mailing and calendar apps. It is easy to use multiple accounts and synchronize them across many devices.
