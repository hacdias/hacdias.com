During 2021, I watched episodes from **52** different shows, as well as
**49** movies. The show I watched the most was **Shadowhunters** with 56 episodes
watched. Regarding movies, I did not repeat any.

The first thing I watched in 2021 was Episode 6 of Season 1 of **The Unlisted**,
on the 2nd of January at 08:39 in the morning; while the last thing I watched 
was Episode 1 of Season 2 of **Travelers**, on the 31st of December at 20:54 in
the evening.
