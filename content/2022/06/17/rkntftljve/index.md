![Book Update Options](https://cdn.hacdias.com/media/3851ec8768588dc2bae84201a17b86e779ae96f48baece43c0808f27bb5410cb.png?caption=false)

However, e-books don't come with a barcode with the ISBN and I'm measuring the progress in percentage - it's also possible to measure in pages though!
