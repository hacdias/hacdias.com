Today Wouter posted about [Finding Stuff on Big Blogs](https://brainbaking.com/post/2022/11/finding-stuff-on-big-blogs/), which was quite an interesting read. I noticed that quite some people had a "on this day" page. It turns out, I already had it implemented, as I can access a post from any day by going to `/YYYY/MM/DD` and if I replace any of those by an `x`, it matches all. So replacing the year by `x` would give me all posts on this day.
