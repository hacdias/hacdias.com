I wouldn't consider "A" to be 20. When using letters, it is complicated to know what is meant by each one of them, unless we know the specific country. It can be both a numerical range (90 to 100%) or the top *x*% of the class like the ECTS grading scheme.

Looking at the USA, A is between 90% to 100% ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academic_grading_in_the_United_States)). That'd be all grades between 18 to 20 and I'm pretty sure there's a lot of high school students in Portugal with those grades and they don't make the news.
