I finished reading the book "O Ano da Morte de Ricardo Reis" today, while on a train from Den Haag to Eindhoven. I will not make a full fledged review of this book, but I want to say a few things: I was not expecting to enjoy this book and it is definitely not a book I am planning to read again. However, I must say that it is quite interesting if you have a background on Fernando Pessoa.
