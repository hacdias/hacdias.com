The Magicians was probably one of the best series I've watched over the last years. It is not very well known, but every one I know that watched it, enjoyed it. As some others, it is based in books. But unlikele most book-based shows, this one is said to be better than the original books.

At some point, I read on the Internet that this is "Harry Potter" for adults. Nevertheless, it is a crazy adventure, filled with magic, love, and unexpected things happening. I agree it may not be for everyone, but I really enjoyed it.
