Hi! As far as I know, everyone must be covered by some insurance, usually the Dutch basic insurance. Only specific cases, such as international students that are not working can be exempt for this, but still need some kind of insurance. If you're from an EU-country, not working and studying, you can be covered by your European Health Insurance card from your origin country. But that's just for emergencies and your country decides what to cover. This was the information given by my university last year.

However, as far as I know, no insurance company can deny you as you're a lawful resident. I'm an international student from a EU country and I arranged my insurance via Independer ([https://www.independer.nl/](https://www.independer.nl/)), which lets you pick by price and features.

I was never asked my job - which is self-employed, but I wasn't at the time - or what I do. Like others mentioned, all adults must have the dutch basic insurance. It's not per household, it's per adult.
