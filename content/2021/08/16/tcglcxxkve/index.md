A comment regarding HPV: if you didn’t yet, I recommend you getting the HPV vaccine. It’s paid and quite expensive but may be worth it. Contact your local GGD (health board).

They’re only going to start giving the vaccine for free to 14 years old boys - like they’ve been doing for girls for years - from 2022 as part of the free immunization program. Sadly, we’re already a bit out of the age group that gets it for free, but we can still pay for it and I think it’s worth it.

Regarding the doctor: not sure. I never had issues with my GP and I don’t really think he cares about my sexual orientation.

Regarding the insurance: if you’re thinking of staying for a while in the Netherlands and if you already pay a lot for AON, I’d consider moving to some Dutch insurance as it is much easier to work with.
