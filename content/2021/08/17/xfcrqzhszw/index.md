Tip for next time if needed: being European, your country is *most likely* part of the [Convention on the Issue of Multilingual Extracts from Civil Status Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Convention_on_the_Issue_of_Multilingual_Extracts_from_Civil_Status_Records). You go wherever they do such thing and ask for an international birth certificate extract and they give you a document in a dozen languages. I paid 15 euros in Portugal for one and was issued in the moment. No one complained about it here in the Netherlands as it's mandatory for them to accept it.

I feel most people don't know about this: the other day I was browsing reddit and someone from a country which issues those extracts was also in search of a translator to pay a huge amount of money to translate a birth certificate in the native language. May not be your case, but if anyone sees this: check if your country issues such thing before asking for a normal birth certificate and save yourself money and time.
