On 2nd August, the directive [2019/1157](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_identity_cards_in_the_European_Economic_Area#New_European_Union_standards) will start being applied across the EU. For some countries, little will change, for others, major ID card redesigns are needed. I'm curious: did your country already reveal the new ID card design?
