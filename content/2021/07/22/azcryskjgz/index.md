You should not need an intermediate service such as PayPal to use your card. McDonalds (and other chains) in many places in Portugal doesn’t even support Maestro/VPay, for example, since it was discontinued in Portugal. It works well for you because you’re in the Netherlands. Once you’re outside, some problems start to arise.

Also, there’s many websites online that do not accept PayPal and only accept card so that wouldn’t work. I just stated some facts. Pretty sure that most people living in the Netherlands don’t have many problems, otherwise the system would’ve been changed by now. It all depends on a person’s needs.

I also want to add that a few years ago, I still had a maestro card from my Portuguese bank, and that card had the numbers and CVC that allowed me to make payments online. I never understood why Dutch maestros don’t have those.

Even you said that 95% of people that have a credit card uses it to pay abroad. Maybe because the Dutch debit cards don’t work everywhere?

Edit: Maestro/VPay networks are cheaper for the merchants and that's probably the reason why the NL didn't move to MasterCard/Visa as their debit card networks. That's also the reason why Portuguese debit cards are dual network: we use Multibanco to be cheaper for Portuguese merchants.
