Yeah, we also have those for years, but they have had the same expiry date as the card, I think. We have two ways of signing: with the card itself (chip) and with an app that they call "Digital Mobile Key" that only requires the card the first time.

However, the new card has NFC, so I think it would be easier since we don't require an actual card reader anymore.

BUT, and it's a big but, apps from the government seem to look and work pretty badly in general. Sometimes, I make complaints and try to help, and I get completely unrelated answers... I'm curious to see how if this new card is going to help/simplify revamping the signing/authenticating process.
