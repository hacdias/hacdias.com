Instead of looking at the 2017 map, you should look into more recent data, such as [2019](https://www.ecdc.europa.eu/sites/default/files/documents/Antimicrobial-consumption-in-the-EU-Annual-Epidemiological-Report-2019.pdf). Yes, Portugal may not be the best but it's below average and you can't get antibiotics without prescription and it's also improving. Figure 3, figure 4, table 6 are enough to show that. Don't jump into conclusions.

Maybe a more useful mindset is to explain your friends - when the subject pops up - what are antibiotics for and how they work, if you feel they get them for everything (which is probably not even possible since most countries require medical prescriptions). Perhaps you do that and people don't listen. But doing so helps in the long run, everyone.
