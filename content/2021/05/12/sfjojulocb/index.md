No worries! It's not your fault most likely. The system is set this way. But the summary is: Visa and Mastercard are credit networks. BUT debit cards can run on top of them and that's what a lot of countries/banks do.

V-Pay and Maestro are also brands from Visa and Mastercard but completely different networks which are strictly debit. Thus, V-Pay and Maestro are cheaper for the merchant. I assume that's the reason why a lot of companies here don't accept Visa and Mastercard: because it's cheaper not to.

However, I think banks - and whoever markets this things - should stop misleading people saying that debit cards cannot be Visa and Mastercard and that you need a credit card. I agree that most don't need a credit card, but a lot of people here have to get one if/when they go for vacations.
