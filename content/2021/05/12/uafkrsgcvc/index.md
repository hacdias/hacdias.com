In Portugal I think you can pay a ticket with card in trains and possibly some other bus lines. I know some train stops don't have tickets so you can either buy them online or when you get into the train. Also, most of the transport systems in Lisbon and Porto are unified within a single card/system. But you have to use it, not the bank card.

If you mean to use the bank card as in London for example, where you can just use it instead of the transports card, no, it's not possible in Portugal as far as I know.
