Probably a brand that usually doesn't come to mind immediately, but that we, Portuguese, usually pronounce wrong is NIVEA. I personally didn't know we pronounced it wrong until I moved to the Netherlands. You can check Google Translator and hear it in Portuguese to see what I said and heard my whole life.
