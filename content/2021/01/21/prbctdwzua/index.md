I'm 21 and I'm Portuguese. I lived my whole life in a very small town. For a matter of perspective, the town has about 800 inhabitants spread over 180 square kilometers (double the size of Lisbon - city, not metropolitan area). Then, I moved to Lisbon to do my Bachelor's degree and it was... amazing! That's literally what I always wanted to do and I really enjoyed all the time until Covid came.

Now, I'm doing my Master's in the Netherlands, in Eindhoven. I'm not in a big city anymore. At least not as big as Lisbon, there's barely any tall buildings. For what I read and learnt, this city was just a collection of villages years ago, so it makes sense.

I have mixed feelings. I love Portugal, even though Portugal has its clear flaws. However, we have an enormous landscape variation across the small country, we have pretty good weather - maybe except winter in the northern areas -, nice people, the sea. Salaries are not high, but rent can be, mainly in Lisbon and Porto and we also have heavy taxes. Another disadvantage is that we're literally in the end of Europe so it's very difficult to travel to other countries by train.

Regarding the Netherlands, it's being an incredible experience. However, I'm just seeing the side with Covid. I've been here since August last year so I haven't experienced much. I love the bike culture. The weather is bad, but it's not *that* bad. At least, when I came here in August it was relatively good. The political system seems to be more transparent and slightly reliable than the Portuguese one (despite made some weird decisions regarding Covid). On the other hand, everything is flat, there's no depth, and I don't really know much about the country since I've never lived it during a "normal" time.

It's complicated. I have absolutely no clue what I want to do after finishing my Master's. I really doubt I will be able to experience this country fully before the end of my master's because the pandemic doesn't seem to be clearing up soon. There's pros and cons in all countries, you can always travel between them. Choose what feels best for you. Maybe I still need to try some other countries. I spend a week in Zurich and I loved it, but it's quite expensive for what it is. We all need some balance.

In the end, I could see myself moving to Lisbon. But I could also see myself moving to some other city in the Netherlands that feels more like a city, like Utrecht, for example. I don't really know. Let's see what the future holds :)

For you, I believe Italy and Portugal are very very very similar. Never been there, but everyone I know that visited or did Erasmus tells me the spirit, the vibes, landscapes, architecture is very similar. Everything you said also happens in Portugal. If you really want to move, try finding a country that meets a balance between all the things you'd like to have on your life.
