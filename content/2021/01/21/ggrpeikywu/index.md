Uh, I decided to do an addendum to myself: I would love to be able to pay for transports with a contactless card, as long as the tariff could be the same. It could even be linked to a monthly payment for those who have those kind of passes. I know TfL (in the UK) allows to use contactless cards for transports.

In Portugal, most of the transport networks are not interconnected (besides Lisbon and Porto) so the systems are always different and you get paper tickets for basically  everything.

I like the OV Chipkaart (similar to the Oyster) here in the NL and the fact that it works on all transports.
