Oh, mine is not a prepaid credit card. It's actually a debit card. Visa is just a network that supports a multitude of cards. The thing is: it's more expensive for the merchant to support Visa. If they support Debit Visa, they are forced to support Credit Visa because it's the same network. On the other hand, Maestro and V-Pay are strictly debit networks and less expensive. I think that might be why a lot of places here don't support Visa and MasterCard.

In Portugal we have something similar: our own network (Multibanco) 😂Portuguese cards are dual network. For example, my PT debit card is Multibanco + Visa. The Portuguese merchants have the option to only accept the national network, which is much much cheaper for them. And some smaller businesses do that.

Still doesn't explain why Albert Heijn doesn't want to accept Visa and MasterCard. Jumbo does, for example, and they're not even as big.

Nevertheless, I have to mention that I never used cash since moving here!

**Edit:** I visited Amsterdam last year in February and paid the ticket with a Mastercard card in the NS machines. I guess they also support credit cards then.
