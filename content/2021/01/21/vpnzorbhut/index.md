In Portugal, I feel there are more and more places accepting cards. However, we have our own national payment network called Multibanco. Usually, all debit cards are dual network: Multibanco + either Visa or MasterCard. There were banks issuing Maestros but they're being discontinued as far as I know.

So, the merchant (usually) has two options: either they only accept the national network, which is cheaper, or they accept everything (Visa, MasterCard, Maestro, V-Pay...), which is more expensive. A lot of establishments only accept the national cards, which makes it annoying for tourists and it is also not contactless compatible.

With that being said, I think there's more and more places getting card readers so that's nice. But (and it's a big but), a lot of places still only accept cash, mainly in smaller towns.

Since I moved to the Netherlands to study, I've never ever used cash again. Well, at least after getting a Dutch account since my Portuguese debit Visa (yes, debit) card was not accepted in Albert Heijn.
