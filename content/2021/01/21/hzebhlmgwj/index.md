My Portuguese debit cards (yes, debit) are Visa and it's really annoying that they are barely accepted across the Netherlands. A lot of people inside the EU that I know also have Visa/MasterCard **debit** cards and it was an hassle to pay for things initially here because we didn't have a BSN yet to open a bank account.

Also, as a note, V-Pay is as much accepted as Maestro in the Netherlands. My ASN debit card is V-Pay.
