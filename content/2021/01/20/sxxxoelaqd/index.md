Hey!

I've been living in the Netherlands since August last year and I opened a bank account with ASN because they seemed to be the most sustainable bank around. However, I am quite worried about their inflexibility regarding English support. I don't mind if the app is in Dutch or the whole website or even the contract. But, I do care about contacting the bank and talking to them in English.

For multiple times, I sent them emails in English, and they replied in Dutch with the following English text in the beginning of the email:

>Thank you for your email. As a Dutch bank, ASN Bank corresponds and services her products in Dutch. We will continue to answer your question in Dutch.

I'm worried that, in the future, if I actually have some issue and need to talk to them over phone, they will act the same way and simply reject speaking English even though they probably can.

Does anyone have any experience with them? Should I switch to a bigger bank?

Thanks

**Edit:** for those suggesting to learn Dutch: I'm doing my master's degree here and English is not my native language either. I've been having Dutch lessons even though I am not even sure if I will stay long in the NL after finishing my degree. The same way it took me years to become fluent in English, it's also unlikely I'll become a fantastic Dutch speaker in a matter of months.
