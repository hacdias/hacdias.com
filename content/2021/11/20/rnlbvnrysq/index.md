Hi y'all!

I've been a self-employed student in the Netherlands since last year. Since I get payments from a single source and there's not a lot of business transactions involved, I did not create a separate account. It is not a legal requirement.

However, my bank, ASN, a few months ago decided to start making questions. I answered them. Now I just received a letter saying I cannot use my bank account from the 3rd of December for business purposes.

In 3 years in Portugal I never got asked a thing. In Portugal, being self-employed is not synonym of having a company like it is here, it's much simpler. And I'm very annoyed. I only get paid on the account, pay my accountant, and pay taxes. I don't see how that is business purposes.

Anyways, does anyone recommend me any bank that is self-employed friendly? I don't want to have two accounts because I only have one source of income and that wouldn't make sense.
