## Ingredients

- 2 to 3 medium ripe peeled bananas
- 76g melted unsalted butter
- 1/2 teaspoon baking soda
- 1 pinch of salt
- 150g sugar
- 1 large egg, beaten
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 205g all-purpose flour

## Preparation

1. Preheat the oven at 175°C
2. Mash the ripe bananas with a fork until completely smooth
3. Stir the melted butter into the mashed bananas.
4. Add the baking soda and salt
5. Stir in the sugar, beaten egg, and vanilla extract.
6. Add the flour.
7. Butter the pan
8. Pour the batter into your prepared loaf pan.
9. Bake for 55 to 65 minutes at 350°F (175°C), or until a toothpick or wooden skewer inserted into the center comes out clean.
