## Ingredients (2 Cups)

* 2 Cups Water
* 2 Cups Whole Milk / Oat Milk
* 10-12 Piece Black Pepper (Whole)
* 5 Piece Cardamom
* 5-6 Pieces Cloves
* 1 Stick Cinnamon
* 1 Inch Ginger
* 1 Tbsp Nuttmeg
* 2 Tbsp Brown Sugar
* 2 Black Tea Bags (= 2 Tsp Black Tea Loose Leafs)

## Preparation

1. Boil 2 cups of water in a cooking pan on medium heat.
2. In a mortar, Take 10-12 pieces of whole pepper, five pieces of Cardamom,5-6 pieces Cloves, and crushed them coarsely with pastel.
3. Take one stick of cinnamon and crushed it.
4. Take 1 inch of ginger and coarsely crushed it.
5. Add all Spices to the boiling water and let them boil for 8 to 10 minutes on medium heat.
6. Add 2 tbsps. Brown sugar to spice water.
7. Take two bags of Black tea and boil in spice water for 2 to 3 minutes until tea fully brew.
8. Strain the tea into two mugs.
9. In another pan boil 2 Cups of whole Milk. Once it starts to boil, turn off the heat.
10. Froth the Milk using a hand blender.
11. Pour the hot frothy Milk in tea and sprinkle a pinch of cinnamon powder.
