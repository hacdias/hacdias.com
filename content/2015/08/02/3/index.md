I've always read books about fictional stories. Fantasy, novels, etc. And this book was the first I read that wasn't fictional. I loved the book and the way Stephen Hawking explains the universe and its history. He is comic and at the same time he is talking seriously. I would recommend it to everyone who wants to get started into the story of Universe and the evolution of the theories.
